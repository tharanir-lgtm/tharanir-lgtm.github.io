Free logical reasoning practice online

Reasoning Room is a free logic practice tool for learners who want to think more clearly. Try a question, choose an answer, and read the explanation to understand the pattern behind it. Questions are generated in your browser from built-in templates; no account is needed, and your progress stays on this device.

Number sequences

Look for a consistent rule between terms: adding the same amount, multiplying, alternating two patterns, or using square numbers.

Example: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, ?
These are 1² through 5², so the next term is 6² = 36.

Verbal analogies

Identify how the first pair is related, then apply the same relationship to the second pair. Relationships can describe purpose, category, part, or place.

Example: Bird is to nest as bee is to hive. Both pairs connect an animal with its home.

Deductive reasoning

Use only the facts in the question. A conclusion must follow from those facts, even if you do not know whether it is true in real life.

Example: All lumes are blickets. All blickets are blue. Therefore, all lumes are blue.

How to get better at logical reasoning

Read the question carefully before looking at the options. For a sequence, compare the gaps between numbers. For an analogy, say the relationship in a short phrase. For a deduction question, connect the statements one step at a time and avoid adding assumptions. After answering, review the explanation and try to describe the rule in your own words.
